# Privacy Policy for AB-Compare Audio Player Pro

**Last updated:** [november 19th, 2025.]

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of your information when you use our application AB-Compare Audio Player Pro.

## Information We Use

The AB-Compare Audio Player Pro application is designed to work entirely offline on your device. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information or data from you or your device.

*   **Audio Files:** The application accesses and processes audio files that you explicitly select from your device's storage. These files are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

*   **Project Data:** Project information, such as the list of audio tracks you use and your Equalizer (EQ) settings, are stored locally in the application's private directory on your device. This data is essential for the application to function and is never transmitted off your device.

## Data We Collect Anonymously

All functionalities of AB-Compare Audio Player Pro, including audio analysis (RTA) and project management, are performed entirely on your device.

To improve the application's stability and features, we use services provided by Google, namely Google Analytics for Firebase. These services help us understand how users interact with the application and identify the sources of crashes or bugs.

The information collected may include, but is not limited to:

* Anonymized device identifiers.
* Application usage statistics (e.g., most used features).
* Crash reports (from Crashlytics), including the device type and operating system version at the time of the crash.

This data is collected anonymously and in an aggregated form. It does not allow us to personally identify you. The data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, which you can review here.

## Permissions

The application may request access to your device's storage. This permission is solely used to allow you to select and load audio files into your projects.
