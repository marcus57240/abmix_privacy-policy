# Privacy Policy for ABMIX

**Last updated:** [november 15th, 2025.]

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of your information when you use our application ABMIX.

## Information We Use

The ABMIX application is designed to work entirely offline on your device. We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information or data from you or your device.

*   **Audio Files:** The application accesses and processes audio files that you explicitly select from your device's storage. These files are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

*   **Project Data:** Project information, such as the list of audio tracks you use and your Equalizer (EQ) settings, are stored locally in the application's private directory on your device. This data is essential for the application to function and is never transmitted off your device.

## No Data Transmission

All functionalities of ABMIX, including audio analysis (RTA) and project management, are performed entirely on your device. We do not have any servers, and we do not collect any analytics, usage statistics, or personal data.

## Permissions

The application may request access to your device's storage. This permission is solely used to allow you to select and load audio files into your projects.
